# Stellar Gear — Design Brief

## Customer price sheet and checkout

The public app stays focused: **choose an item, set a quantity, see the total, and pay with Bitcoin**. It keeps the user-supplied Stellar Gear black, red, and metallic identity while avoiding store jargon and visual clutter.

- One clear list of customer-editable items, price per line, quantity controls, and a running item total.
- Products that are manually unavailable or have zero known inventory are clearly marked **Out of stock** and cannot be added.
- A straightforward checkout form asks only for the information needed to ship: name, email, phone, and address.
- A dark Bitcoin payment screen makes the next action unmistakable: scan the QR code or copy the address, send the exact BTC amount, then watch the status progress to a thank-you confirmation.
- The $20 shipping charge is named plainly in checkout—not hidden in the item total.

## Password-protected admin workspace

`/admin` is an operational workspace behind a dedicated dashboard password—not the public price sheet.

- A simple password entry screen, with no product details exposed until access is granted.
- A compact inventory summary and table focused on **Item / Price / Inventory / Status**.
- A direct form for adding and editing products, including price, sale price, inventory count, and manual availability.
- Clear red out-of-stock treatment and neutral metallic status treatment.

## Visual direction

- Near-black workspace background, warm-white price sheet, metallic-silver table accents, and signal-red action/status highlights.
- The supplied Stellar Gear bodybuilding logo anchors the public sheet, payment screen, and admin workspace.
- Direct sans-serif text with a restrained condensed uppercase title; no jargon.

## Brand voice

**Clear, quiet, and practical.** The interface tells a customer exactly what to pay and tells the owner only what they need to manage products and inventory.
