# Stellar Gear — Design Brief

## Chosen direction: Fireline Utility

A premium, high-contrast ordering desk that combines the confidence of a performance brand with the clarity of a modern operations interface. The direction follows the confirmed **fire-red, black, and white** identity and keeps the experience catalog-neutral, fast to scan, and easy to manage.

### Design movement
A restrained performance-luxury system: editorial type scale, near-black surfaces, sharp modules, and technical labeling. The customer order page feels like an elevated gear locker; the admin view feels like a clean, practical control room.

### Core principles
- Make choices and totals instantly legible.
- Put the order summary in reach without competing with the product list.
- Use red purposefully for primary actions, sale states, and operational emphasis.
- Keep every form field and management action visibly structured and touch-friendly.

### Color philosophy
- **Carbon black** is the main canvas (#0A0A0B), creating a focused, premium foundation.
- **Signal red** is the signature action color (#F13B31), reinforced by a deeper ember red for surfaces.
- **Warm white** (#F8F7F3) carries primary type and bright panels.
- **Steel gray** and muted white support metadata, borders, and hierarchy.

### Layout paradigm
A wide editorial storefront with a compact utility header, a large introduction area, a responsive product grid, and a sticky desktop order panel. Mobile collapses the order panel into an accessible bottom summary. The admin surface reuses the same identity but prioritizes dense, calm operational controls.

### Signature elements
- A compact Stellar Gear emblem: a four-point stellar flare cut through a red gear-like aperture.
- Hairline borders, small uppercase utility labels, and slim red rule accents.
- Product cards with index numbers, category markers, clear price typography, and restrained state pills.
- A large, rounded order card with an analog-style total readout and live item count.

### Interaction philosophy
Immediate and reversible. Quantity controls update totals at once; unavailable products are visually clear and cannot be added; administrative edits open focused forms with obvious save and cancel choices. Success feedback is brief, specific, and non-blocking.

### Animation
Subtle only: short fade-and-rise entrance on page sections, lightweight hover elevation, and a restrained total pulse when the order changes. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography system
A condensed display voice for brand moments and section numerals, paired with an exceptionally readable geometric sans for products, controls, and customer details. Utility text uses deliberate uppercase spacing.

### Brand essence and voice
**Disciplined. Direct. Charged.** Stellar Gear speaks like a high-performance outfitter: concise, confident, and practical. It never over-explains or uses medical, pharmaceutical, cryptocurrency, or payment-processing language.

### Wordmark and logo
The wordmark is **STELLAR GEAR** in compact uppercase, paired with a simple red stellar-flare/gear-aperture emblem. The logo must work as a favicon, header mark, and square project icon without relying on small details or generated typography.

### Signature brand color
**Signal Red — #F13B31**

### Planned visual asset
One opaque square project icon: an abstract red stellar flare cut into a black gear aperture, centered on a full-bleed black background with no text, badge, gradient, glow, shadow, or rounded outer canvas.
